Who Needs This Farm Safety Course?

This farm health and safety course is for farmers who need to meet TAMS grant safety requirements, those required to complete farm safety training, or anyone who needs an updated certificate. It's also suitable for new farm owners and young farmers entering the sector. The certificate is valid for 5 years.

What the Course Covers

Practical topics designed for working farms

The course covers the essential safety topics you need for your farm. Topics include:

  • Farm risk assessment
  • Machinery and equipment safety
  • Livestock handling
  • Slurry and confined spaces
  • Working at height
  • Personal safety on farms
